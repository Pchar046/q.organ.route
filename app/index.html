Q-Organ Route

AI predicts flight delays from real U.S. flight data. A quantum optimizer (QAOA) picks charter jet, commercial flight, or ground for each organ so every organ arrives inside its cold-ischemia limit. When something goes wrong, the plan is solved again.

The quantum results are real. Our QAOA ran on IonQ's Aria 1 noise-model simulator via Qollab and found the best plan in every recorded run. A web page can't call IonQ live, so the sliders check each new plan instantly with an exact solver, which confirms the same answers QAOA found. Times, costs and limits are illustrative planning values, not clinical guidance.

Measuring failures

How often does an organ miss its time limit?

Runs 10,000 random transports on the normal-day schedule. Each flight can run late using the AI's delay odds, and in about 1 of 10 transports the jet reports a 1–3 hour delay before departure. Every plan faces the same random delays.
