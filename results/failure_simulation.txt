Q-Organ Route - transport failure simulation (failure_sim.py)
Date: Oct 10, 2026 (build window)
Failure = an organ arrives after its cold-ischemia limit (heart 4 h, liver 12 h, kidney 24 h).
Assumptions: jet delayed before departure in 10% of transports, by 1-3 h. Late legs draw an
exponential delay with the leg's mean. Commercial-flight risk from the AI model (BTS Dec 2025).

Delay risk source: AI model on BTS Dec 2025 data
Transports simulated: 10,000  (jet delayed before departure in ~10%)
Basic dispatch plan:      {'heart': 'commercial', 'liver': 'ground', 'kidney': 'ground'}
Q-Organ Route plan:       {'heart': 'jet', 'liver': 'commercial', 'kidney': 'commercial'}
Re-planned in 1,033 transports
